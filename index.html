Celestial rules & planetary rulers

Conjunction: four or more matching signs connected horizontally or vertically clear and cascade. A cluster needs at least two actual signs. A matching ruler may substitute for the signs it governs (celestial dignity). Shared wilds count only once, favoring larger matches. Conjunction base points for 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 matching symbols are 4, 12, 30, 70, 150 and 300; each additional symbol adds 200. A match containing a ruler earns 1.5× base points. Cascade multipliers double: ×1, ×2, ×4, ×8, then ×16 maximum.

Grand trine: when ordinary matches are exhausted, a connected group of at least six zodiac symbols containing all three signs of one element can clear once per spin, earning two points per symbol before the cascade multiplier. Solar flare: a Sun in a Leo match clears its neighboring tiles.

Lunar cycle: three Moons on the initial board grant five free spins. Grand conjunction: five distinct planetary rulers on the initial board unlock the zodiac wheel. Every four spins also earn a Zodiac Wheel turn. These are game mechanics inspired by traditional astrology, not literal horoscope rules.

Planetary powers: the wheel can award Mercury (swap adjacent tiles), Saturn (seal a tile through your next spin), Mars (clear one tile), Venus (pull the nearest matching sign beside a chosen sign), Jupiter (double the next scoring alignment), or Moon (change a tile to any zodiac sign). Select an earned power, then tap the board. Each use spends one charge; cancelling is free. Sealed tiles stay fixed and may help a match, but cannot be cleared or moved until the next spin ends. Unused powers and wheel turns are saved with your current session on this browser. Starting a new session resets them, but keeps your lifetime statistics and high score.

Twenty regular spins per session, plus free spins. Points have no monetary value. No purchases, cash redemption or prizes.
